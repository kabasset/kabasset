Publications
============

As a Euclid Builder, I am allowed to sign any Euclid publication.
In addition to the mission reference papers, I decided to only sign research for which I had significant impact.
Typically, ERO papers rely heavily on LE1, and EleFits was initially designed for SIR and SPE needs.

This page lists selected papers and is not updated daily.
For a more exhaustive list, refer to `my Research Gate profile <https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Antoine-Basset/research>`_.
