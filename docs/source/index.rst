Welcome to my personal website!
I am a software engineer focused on optimization, parallelization and efficiency.
On the research side, I have a doctorate in signal processing
and supervise students and PostDocs in computer science and machine learning.

As an employee of `CNES <https://cnes.fr/en>`_, the French space agency,
I mostly work in the frame of large space missions like Euclid or Lisa
at the interface between astronomy, signal processing and computer science.
Over the various projects I have be involved in,
I participated to all the stages in the life of a science ground segment,
from research phases and early design to operations and public outreach.
Previously, I used to work in the biomedical field as an intern (epidemiology) and PhD student (cell biology).
When I was sooo young, I have also studied and spent a year in the aeronautical industry.
